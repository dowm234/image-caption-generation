Features & How to Use

1. Upload Image

Select an image from your device to generate captions. Supported formats include JPG, PNG, and GIF.

2. AI Caption Generation

Our AI model (BLIP-2) analyzes your image and produces creative, engaging captions that match the content and style.

3. Caption History

Every generated caption is saved automatically in your history. Revisit your previous captions and reuse them anytime.

4. Tips for Best Results

  • Try different angles and scenes to get diverse captions.
  • Combine captions with hashtags or emojis for social media impact.
